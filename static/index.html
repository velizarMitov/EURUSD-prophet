EURUSD Directional Extrapolator

Fully autonomous — knows today's date itself, fetches live market data (MT5, falling back to Yahoo Finance), and runs two model variants side by side: a price-only baseline and an experimental with-macro-features variant (each a GBM + LSTM committee), plus the auxiliary H1→Daily intraday ensemble (XGBoost, Random Forest, SVM, LSTM). No manual input, no date picker.
