   READ THIS CORRECTLY: the extended panels carry-vs-majority delta is LARGER
   than the primary's (+0.0675 vs +0.0279) even though carry itself is LESS accurate there (0.5197 vs 0.5292). The gap
   widens because the REFERENCE collapses (0.4522 vs 0.5013): MXN and ZAR trend down against the dollar,
   so a train-majority baseline that predicts UP is badly wrong on them. That is
   a weaker baseline, not a stronger signal, and it is exactly why this arm was
   pre-registered as descriptive and never as a path to KEEP.
   Agreement with the primary: both arms DROP the logistic against carry, and
   both show carry above its baseline; they do not point in opposite directions.
