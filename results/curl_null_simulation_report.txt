Theory says realised/theory ~ 1.0 (closed form is exact up to the exponential
approximation of the backward recurrence time), the BINNED slope of c^2 on the
feed-only proxy P ~ 0.5, and out-of-sample mean excess X ~ 1.0.
Note corr(c2,P) ~ 0.1 EVEN THOUGH THE NULL IS EXACTLY TRUE: conditional on P,
c^2 is P*chi^2_1, and that multiplicative noise attenuates the correlation to
about CV(P)/sqrt(2). Never judge the null by the raw per-bar correlation.

RMS curl FLAT in bar size, RMS return ~ sqrt(D). On the real feed:

  rising with D   -> real persistent dislocation OR a stale feed; separate the

==================================================================================
C3. Shift placebo — how much misalignment would explain the curl?
==================================================================================
  shifting EURUSD by 1 bar inflates RMS curl x9.2
  shifting GBPJPY by 1 bar inflates RMS curl x12.3

One bar of misalignment is enormous relative to a few seconds of staleness.
If your observed curl is anywhere near this scale, you have an alignment bug,
not a discovery.
